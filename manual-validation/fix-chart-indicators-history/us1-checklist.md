# US1 Checklist - Chart Legibility

## Scenarios
- [ ] Month labels do not overlap bottom border
- [ ] Title-to-legend vertical spacing is between 8px and 16px
- [ ] Y-axis labels do not repeat redundantly for low values
- [ ] Y-axis behaves correctly with single-point or near-zero ranges
- [ ] Chart remains legible on desktop and mobile
